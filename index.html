Multi-stop route planner · iPhone & Android

Plan your whole day of stops. Tap once. Go.

WayPoint puts your errands, showings, deliveries, and road-trip stops in the best order, then hands each one to the navigation app you already use. No sign-up. No account.

Everything a multi-stop day needs

Built for everyday errands, real-estate showings, food road trips, and anyone with more than one place to be.

📲

Add stops from any app

Tap Share in Google Maps, Apple Maps, Zillow, Realtor.com, Yelp, Contacts, or a text message and pick WayPoint. The address comes right in.

✨

One-tap Optimize

WayPoint puts your stops in an efficient order and shows the distance and drive time to each one.

▶️

Navigate your way

Each stop opens in Google Maps, Apple Maps, or Waze. Come back and it's checked off, with the next stop ready.

📍

Saved Places

Keep the places you go often and add them to any trip with one tap.

🗺️

Saved Routes

Save a whole day's route and load it again next week.

🔒

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your stops and places stay on your phone.

How it works

Three steps from a pile of addresses to a finished day.

1

Plan

Share stops into WayPoint from the apps you already use, or type them in.

2

Tap

Tap Optimize to put everything in the best order.

3

Go

Tap Navigate, follow your directions, and check stops off as you go. Return to Origin brings you home.
