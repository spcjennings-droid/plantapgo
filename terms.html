2. What WayPoint Does

WayPoint is a multi-stop route planning app for Android and iPhone. It lets you:

  • Plan trips with multiple stops and put them in an efficient order
  • Add stops by typing them or sharing them from other apps
  • Save frequently used addresses as Saved Places and reuse routes as Saved Routes
  • Track progress by checking off completed stops, and review your Stop History
  • Share routes and places with others using a link
  • Open your navigation app (such as Google Maps, Apple Maps, or Waze) for turn-by-turn directions

WayPoint plans your stops. Your navigation app chooses the roads and provides the turn-by-turn directions. WayPoint does not provide turn-by-turn navigation itself.
